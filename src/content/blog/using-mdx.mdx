## How did you build this website?

I do nothing without God's help, and some good ol' fashioned elbow grease.

## What tools are you using, and what are you paying to keep this website running?

I only pay for two things: a monthly ChatGPT subscription and a small annual domain fee for tomweglarz.com (something like $12 annually).

ChatGPT saves me significant time by helping with coding and site edits. I’ve stuck with it for its simple layout, and for its ability to organize conversation into separate projects.

The site is hosted for free using GitHub and Cloudflare. I edit and manage the site through GitHub, copying and pasting back and forth from chatgpt into github, and githubinto ChatGPT. ChatGPT tells me what sections to edit and how.

## Where did this black and white theme/design come from? How can I also use it for free?

This theme is called Dasein, and it is offered through a group/company called "Astro" website builds. It comes with the [@astrojs/mdx](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/integrations-guide/mdx/) integration installed and configured in your `astro.config.mjs` config file. If you prefer not to use MDX, you can disable support by removing the integration from your config file.
